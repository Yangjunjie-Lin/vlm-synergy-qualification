# Project identity

CapabilityGate is a new model-cohort qualification project. Its sole question
is whether at least two of three prospectively frozen open-weight VLM families
have direct visual-relation, text-relation, reversal, bridge-binding, and
minimal joint-composition capability under two frozen answer contracts.

The project is not SynergyTrace, is not an amendment to SynergyTrace, and does
not estimate a circuit, layer, head, or causal mechanism. Qualification data
may select models for later work but may never serve as confirmatory evidence
in that work.
