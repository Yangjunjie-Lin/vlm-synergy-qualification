# Claim boundary

Allowed claims are limited to measured atomic capability, current-cohort
eligibility, joint behavior relative to unimodal controls, and whether a new
held-out mechanism preregistration is warranted.

The screen cannot establish a cross-modal circuit, localize composition,
identify an attention head, generalize a selected model to all VLMs, or turn a
qualification failure into evidence that cross-modal synergy does not exist.
No activation patching is implemented or authorized in this repository.
